Traid — designed & developed by Xavier Sun
==========================================

Traid replaces short text in photos (labels, serial numbers, painted or engraved
lettering) using real characters learned from your own reference photos. Everything
runs on this computer: no account, no cloud, no telemetry.

Acceptable use
--------------
By installing Traid you agree to:

  * Edit only images that you own or are authorised to change.
  * Not use Traid to forge or alter documents, IDs, money, certificates, receipts
    or records, or to deceive anyone about what an image shows.
  * Keep exported files honest: Traid marks exports as edited by default and never
    writes camera, date-taken or location data into edited images.

Software
--------
Traid uses open-source components, including PP-OCRv4 (PaddleOCR, Apache-2.0),
LaMa inpainting (Apache-2.0), ONNX Runtime (MIT), OpenCV (Apache-2.0), Pillow,
NumPy, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, React and pywebview. Their licences apply to them.

Your data is stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Traid and is kept when Traid is uninstalled,
so a reinstall finds your profiles again. Delete that folder to remove it.
